A Crowded Mind

 

Secrets in small towns rarely stay buried.

 

Ethan Cross is a damn good homicide cop. But what makes him such a good cop is the one thing that, if it unravels, will take him down with it.

 

For more than two decades, Ethan has been trying to keep his secrets buried without even knowing they were there. But a recent string of seemingly random murders has been making that harder and harder. As the lines begin to blur, Ethan finds himself questioning not only the investigation, but his own mind.

 

At its heart, The Crowded Mind series is a story about survival. It explores the lengths a child will go to endure the unbearable, the scars those choices leave behind, and the long road toward healing.

 

"It's about surviving the things that should have destroyed you, and what happens when you finally have to face them."
