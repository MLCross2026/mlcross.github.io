About M.L. Cross

M.L. Cross lives in a small Alabama town that never saw much reason to leave the 1970s behind—and honestly, she is perfectly fine with that. A child of the ’70s herself, she had stories taking shape in her mind long before encouragement from close family and friends gave her the push to follow them. She has been creating characters ever since, usually giving them complicated minds, questionable decisions, and enough darkness to keep things interesting. Her fascination with the human psyche influences her writing, while her very dark sense of humor has a habit of slipping into places it probably should not. When she is not writing, she is usually reading, trying to convince her pets that she is the one in charge, or working on restoring her (you guessed it) 1970s home.
